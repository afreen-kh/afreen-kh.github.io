I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Application at Integral University, Lucknow, where I conduct research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, healthcare informatics, and clinical decision support. My work focuses on developing AI systems that integrate diverse healthcare data, from neuroimaging and genomic biomarkers to clinical narratives and social determinants of health, to advance precision medicine and address health equity challenges in resource-limited settings.

## Research Portfolio

My research integrates machine learning, natural language processing, and geospatial analytics to solve critical problems in healthcare. I develop explainable, bias-aware AI systems across three main areas: neurodegenerative disease diagnosis, large-scale population health surveillance, and clinical text mining. My approach emphasizes translational research that bridges academic innovation with practical healthcare deployment.

### Neurodegenerative Disease Prediction & Diagnosis

I developed ADNeuroNet (2025), a neuroevolution-based neural network for Alzheimer's disease diagnosis that integrates MRI neuroimaging, clinical assessments, cognitive test scores, and protein biomarker data. My parallel machine learning architecture correlates AT(N) protein biomarkers with dementia-associated drug usage patterns, published in *Frontiers in Neuroscience* (2024). This work demonstrates how multimodal data fusion can improve diagnostic accuracy and provide insights into disease mechanisms.

### Large-Scale Population Health Analytics

I lead the SMART-Rural project, analyzing health data from 17,773 individuals across rural Lucknow District. Using machine learning (Random Forest, XGBoost, ensemble methods) combined with geospatial analytics (Moran's I, Getis-Ord Gi*, Geographic Weighted Regression), I predict chronic disease risk and identify geographic health disparities. This work integrates social determinants of health, education, income, sanitation, healthcare access, with spatial analysis to inform targeted public health interventions in underserved populations. At the IAPSM UP-UK Conference (November 2025), I delivered an oral research presentation on the SMART-Rural framework and was invited to present a lecture covering both SMART-Rural applications and HealthDS (a synthetic healthcare data generation platform for India). Based on this dataset, multiple research studies are currently underway with manuscripts in preparation for submission to international journals as well.

### Synthetic Healthcare Data Generation for India (HealthDS)

I developed HealthDS, a framework for generating India-specific synthetic healthcare data based on National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5) patterns. This tool addresses the critical gap in locally-relevant healthcare datasets by incorporating Indian demographic distributions, district-level health indicators, and public health infrastructure characteristics. HealthDS enables generation of realistic synthetic data for AI research while ensuring privacy compliance—particularly valuable for developing and testing healthcare AI models in the Indian context. I presented this work as an invited lecture at the IAPSM UP-UK Conference (2024), and a manuscript is currently in preparation.

### Clinical Natural Language Processing & Biomedical Text Mining

I am developing biomedical named entity recognition (NER) models for automated extraction of medical entities from unstructured electronic health records. My recent work on LLM-based clinical text summarization (presented at IEEE ICCCNT 2025 and ICIVC 2025) explores how large language models can optimize clinical insights from narrative documentation. This research addresses the critical challenge of converting unstructured clinical notes into structured, actionable data for decision support systems.

### AI for Mental Health & Recovery Resilience

As Principal Investigator on a funded project (₹2 lakhs, 2025-2027), I am developing AI frameworks for building recovery resilience in severe mental illness by analyzing social determinants across urban and rural populations. This work combines predictive modeling with environmental health factors and clinical assessments to identify individuals at risk and inform early intervention strategies.
