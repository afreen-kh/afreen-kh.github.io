I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Application at Integral University, Lucknow, where I lead research at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, healthcare informatics, and clinical decision support**. My work focuses on developing multimodal AI systems that integrate clinical imaging, electronic health records, and social determinants of health to advance precision medicine and health equity.

## Research Focus: Multimodal Agentic AI for Healthcare

My research centers on building **intelligent, autonomous AI systems** that can reason across diverse healthcare data modalities—from neuroimaging and genomic data to clinical narratives and population health indicators. I develop frameworks that combine **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**, **multimodal learning**, and **agentic architectures** to create explainable, fair, and privacy-preserving AI tools for clinical decision support and disease prediction.

### Key Research Areas

**Neurodegenerative Disease Prediction & Diagnosis**  
Developed ADNeuroNet, a neuroevolution-based neural network achieving state-of-the-art performance in Alzheimer's disease diagnosis through multimodal fusion of MRI imaging, clinical assessments, and biomarker data. My work on parallel machine learning architectures correlates AT(N) protein biomarkers with dementia-associated drug usage patterns, advancing our understanding of disease progression.

**Large-Scale Population Health Analytics**  
Leading the SMART-Rural project, analyzing health data from 17,773 individuals across rural Lucknow District using machine learning and geospatial analytics to predict chronic disease risk. This work integrates social determinants of health with spatial analysis to identify geographic health disparities and inform targeted public health interventions in underserved populations.

**Clinical Natural Language Processing & LLM Applications**  
Developing biomedical NER models and LLM-based systems for automated extraction of medical entities from unstructured electronic health records, with applications in clinical text summarization and decision support. Current work explores retrieval-augmented generation for evidence-based clinical reasoning grounded in medical literature.

**AI for Mental Health & Recovery Resilience**  
Principal Investigator on a funded project developing AI-driven frameworks for building recovery resilience in severe mental illness by analyzing social determinants across urban and rural populations. This work combines predictive modeling, clinical text analysis, and environmental health factors.
