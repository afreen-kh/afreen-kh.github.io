Neuroevolution-based neural network integrating MRI neuroimaging, clinical assessments, cognitive test scores, and protein biomarker data for Alzheimer disease diagnosis. The approach demonstrates how multimodal data fusion can improve diagnostic accuracy and provide insights into disease mechanisms.
