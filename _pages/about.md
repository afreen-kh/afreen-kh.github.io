I am an Assistant Professor at Integral University, Lucknow, specializing in **Healthcare AI, Machine Learning, and Explainable AI**. My research focuses on developing AI systems that address real-world healthcare challenges, with particular emphasis on disease prediction, biological aging, and health equity in low-resource settings.

## Research Highlights

**SMART-Rural Project:** Leading analysis of 17,773 individuals across rural Lucknow District, combining machine learning and geospatial analytics to predict chronic disease risk and identify geographic health disparities. This work demonstrates how AI can inform targeted public health interventions in underserved populations.

**Biological Aging Research:** Developing multi-agent federated learning systems for biological age prediction across 180,000+ individuals from diverse global populations (LASI, SAGE, NHANES), enabling privacy-preserving analysis across international datasets.

**HealthDS Platform:** Creating India-specific synthetic healthcare data generation tools to address the critical gap in locally-relevant datasets for AI research and development in public health.

**Explainable AI:** Building frameworks (AXAI-TD, SREC) that make AI systems transparent and interpretable for clinicians, patients, and policymakers, ensuring responsible deployment of healthcare AI.

## Current Projects

- **Smart ICU System:** Developing IoT-based real-time patient monitoring with predictive analytics
- **Integrated Hospital Management System (IHMS):** Building modern web-based hospital management platform
- **Label Noise Correction (SREC):** Cost-aware approaches to handling noisy medical imaging labels
- **Agentic AI in Healthcare:** Exploring autonomous AI agents for clinical decision support
