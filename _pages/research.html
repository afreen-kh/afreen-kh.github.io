Research at a Glance

  • Multimodal AI for neurodegenerative disease diagnosis, integrating neuroimaging, clinical, and cognitive data
  • Population-scale health risk modeling using data-driven and geospatial analytics
  • Privacy-aware synthetic health data generation for healthcare research and education
  • Clinical natural language processing and large language models for healthcare decision support

My work emphasizes algorithmic rigor, interpretability, and deployment-aware design, enabling AI systems that translate complex healthcare data into actionable clinical and population-level insights.

NLP and large language models for extracting and summarizing clinical information from unstructured records.
Mental Health Analytics & Resilience Modeling
AI-based modeling of recovery trajectories and resilience in severe mental illness.
