I am an AI and machine learning researcher working at the intersection of healthcare, clinical data science, and population health. My work focuses on developing explainable, data-driven models that translate complex biomedical and public health data into actionable clinical and policy insights. I am currently an Assistant Professor at Integral University, Lucknow, where I combine research, teaching, and academic leadership with active involvement in applied healthcare technology development.

My research spans multiple scales of healthcare data—from neuroimaging and clinical assessments at the individual level to large-scale population and geospatial health analytics. A central theme of my work is multimodal data integration, particularly for neurodegenerative disease prediction. I developed **ADNeuroNet (2025)**, a neuroevolution-based deep learning framework for Alzheimer’s disease diagnosis that integrates MRI neuroimaging, cognitive assessments, and clinical features. In parallel, my work has examined the relationship between AT(N) protein biomarkers and dementia-related medication usage, demonstrating how machine learning can reveal clinically meaningful patterns across heterogeneous data sources.

Beyond disease-specific modeling, I lead research in population health analytics through the **SMART-Rural** project, which analyzes health data from over 17,000 individuals across rural regions of Lucknow District. This work combines machine learning methods (including ensemble models and gradient boosting) with geospatial analytics to study chronic disease risk, health disparities, and the role of social determinants such as education, income, sanitation, and healthcare access. Findings from this project have been presented at national and international public health forums, and multiple manuscripts based on this dataset are currently in preparation.

To address the limited availability of realistic, privacy-preserving healthcare datasets in India, I developed **HealthDS**, a context-aware synthetic health data generation framework grounded in National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5) patterns. HealthDS enables the creation of demographically and geographically representative synthetic datasets for healthcare AI research while maintaining data privacy—supporting both education and early-stage model development in resource-constrained settings.

My work also extends into clinical natural language processing, where I develop biomedical named entity recognition and large language model–based approaches for extracting and summarizing information from unstructured electronic health records. This research aims to bridge the gap between narrative clinical documentation and structured decision-support systems, improving the usability of real-world clinical data.

In addition to research, I serve as **Technical Coordinator for the Integrated Healthcare Management System (IHMS)** at Integral Institute of Medical Sciences & Research. In this role, I work closely with clinicians and software teams on system design, workflow modeling, and real-world deployment of digital healthcare infrastructure—experience that directly informs my research in applied clinical AI.

I am actively engaged in academic service and leadership, serving as an **Editorial Board Member for Scientific Reports (Springer Nature)**, a **Session Chair at international conferences**, and an **organizing committee member** for major academic events. I also continue to expand my interdisciplinary expertise through advanced training in climate-health informatics, currently pursuing a **Post-Graduate Diploma in Data Science in Health and Climate Change for Social Impact at IIIT Delhi**.

My long-term goal is to advance equitable, data-driven healthcare systems by integrating machine learning, clinical science, and population health—ensuring that AI innovations are not only methodologically rigorous but also socially relevant and deployable in real-world healthcare settings.
