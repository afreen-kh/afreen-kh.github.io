Neurodegenerative Disease Modeling & Clinical AI

ADNeuroNet: Neuroevolution-based Deep Learning for Alzheimer’s Disease Diagnosis

Multimodal Clinical AI · Neuroimaging · Biomarkers
Developed a neuroevolution-based deep learning framework integrating MRI neuroimaging, cognitive assessments, and clinical features for Alzheimer’s disease diagnosis. Published in Neural Computing and Applications (2025).

Paper | Code

Biomarker–Medication Interaction Modeling in Dementia

Machine Learning · Protein Biomarkers · Clinical Data
Machine learning analysis of AT(N) protein biomarkers and dementia-related medication usage, highlighting clinically meaningful associations across heterogeneous biomedical data. Published in Frontiers in Neuroscience (2024).

Paper

Population Health Analytics & Health Equity

SMART-Rural: Geospatial AI for Chronic Disease Risk Stratification

Population Health · Geospatial Analytics · Social Determinants
Large-scale population health study analyzing data from over 17,000 individuals across rural regions of Lucknow District. Combines ensemble machine learning models with geospatial analytics to identify chronic disease risk, spatial clustering, and healthcare access disparities.

Project | GitHub

Socio-Geographic Risk Modeling for Public Health Intervention

Health Equity · Risk Modeling
Machine learning models incorporating education, income, sanitation, and healthcare access to support targeted public health interventions in underserved populations.

Synthetic Healthcare Data & Privacy-Aware AI

HealthDS: Context-Aware Synthetic Health Data Generation

Synthetic Data · Privacy-Preserving AI
Developed a synthetic healthcare data generation framework grounded in NFHS-5 patterns, enabling realistic, demographically representative datasets for healthcare AI research while ensuring privacy compliance.

GitHub
